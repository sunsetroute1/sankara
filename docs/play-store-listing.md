## Full description

Turn any photo, drawing, or card into a crisp image on a battery-free NFC e-paper display.

Sankara is the companion app for Waveshare-style NFC-powered e-paper modules. Hold your phone to the display’s NFC coil and your image appears in seconds—no charging, no Bluetooth pairing, and no account required. Mount the panel on a bag, desk stand, locker, instrument, or anywhere you want a calm, always-visible surface that only updates when you choose.

**What you can show**
• Photos and artwork — import from your gallery or receive shares from any app (Quick Share, Nearby Share, messaging, and more)
• E-ink tuned edits — crop to your panel size, adjust contrast, threshold, and dithering so previews match what the display will show
• Text cards — compose clean typographic layouts for quotes, labels, and reminders
• Hand-drawn graphics — sketch directly in the app
• Card Studio — QR codes, Wi‑Fi login cards, contact cards, and multi-link cards built for e-ink readability
• Now Playing — mirror the track on your phone to a minimal e-ink “now playing” card (requires notification access)

**Built for real hardware**
• Automatic display discovery over NFC — panel size, color mode, and driver selection
• Optimized transfer for Waveshare NFC e-paper modules (including 2.7" 264×176 panels)
• Reflash your last image anytime without re-editing
• Clear sync progress and guidance when placement needs adjustment

**Why NFC e-paper?**
The display holds your image without power. Your phone supplies energy only during the brief sync. That makes these modules ideal for badges, shelf tags, art pieces, instrument panels, workshop labels, and everyday objects you want to personalize—retrofit the panel wherever it fits your project.

**Requirements**
• Android phone with NFC
• Compatible NFC e-paper module (Waveshare NFC e-paper series and similar)
• Notification access optional — only needed for Now Playing
